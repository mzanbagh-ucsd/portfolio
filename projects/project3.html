    How to read the charts

    Lines show life expectancy over time. Bars show how much it changed between the years you choose. Click a bar to add that country to the line chart. The chart compares up to five countries at once.

    The data covers 1960–2020. Life-expectancy values come from the World Bank; country regions come from the GitHub dataset. A country appears in the ranking only when both years have data. Missing values are not filled in.

    Data and process

    I completed the project on my own with instructor approval. I estimate it would take a student about 12–16 hours without AI help, mostly to prepare the data and connect the chart controls. This is an estimate, not a tracked time log.

    Life-expectancy data: World Bank API. Country regions: World Bank Data by Indicators. A copy of the data is included with the project.
